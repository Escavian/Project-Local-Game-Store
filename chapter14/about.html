About Gaming Dragon

Our Storied Adventure

Pioneered in 2025 by lifelong gamers, we are excited to open our doors on February 23, 2026 at 9:00a.m. to finally serve the local gaming community. A welcoming space for players of all ages and experience levels coming together to learn, play, and discover new games.

What Gaming Dragon Offers

We specialize and offer board games, trading card games, tabletop RPGs, miniatures, and accessories. Whether you're a casual player or a competitive strategist, we carry something for every type of gamer.

Our Quest

Our goal here at Gaming Dragon is to create a fun, thriving all inclusive environment where everyone feels welcome. We host weekly events, tournaments, and casual open play nights to help players connect and build lasting friendships.

Why Recommend Gaming Dragon?

Founded by locals who grew up playing at other neighborhood stores, we believe we are more than just another shop—we are the community! Our passionate and knowledgeable staff are always eager to help you find your next favorite game. We pride ourselves on being the guiding hand every adventurer needs.
